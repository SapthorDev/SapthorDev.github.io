02 / Agent loop

Four steps.
One mental model.

A good agent product should make autonomy feel understandable. Relay keeps the entire loop legible instead of hiding everything behind a single chat box.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Start with the goal.

Relay identifies the outcome, context, constraints and missing information before taking action.

02 / PLAN

Make the path visible.

The goal becomes a short plan with clear actions, dependencies and moments that need human approval.

03 / EXECUTE

Handle the repetitive work.

Relay works through the plan while keeping progress, blockers and actions visible in the workspace.

04 / REPORT

Return with clarity.

You get the result, what changed, what still needs attention and the evidence behind the work.

03 / Product principles

Useful autonomy.
Visible control.

This concept focuses on the parts of agent UX that matter after the first demo: speed, repetitive execution and confidence that the system is doing what you intended.

R

Research faster

Collect and compress information into the decisions that actually matter instead of another pile of tabs.

A

Automate busywork

Move repetitive digital tasks out of your day while preserving enough visibility to trust the workflow.

C

Stay in control

Plans, progress, approvals and results remain visible so autonomy never has to mean ambiguity.
