Social product / Case study

Zuvoqi

A social product built around curiosity, guesses and delayed reveals — with a product loop that gives people a reason to come back.

Product designUX statesSecurity thinkingSocial mechanics
01 / PROBLEM

What needed solving

Most social interactions resolve immediately. This concept needed a clear way to create curiosity now while preserving a reason to return later.

02 / BUILD

What I built

I designed the Mystery → guess → sealed truth → reveal flow and built the surrounding states, access rules and interactions around that mechanic.

03 / VALUE

What this demonstrates

It demonstrates product thinking beyond screens: user loops, state design, timing, edge cases and the logic behind the experience.

Proof of thinking

Not just a screenshot.

Distinct product loopThe core interaction is not a standard feed or like mechanic.
State-heavy UXGuessing, waiting and revealing each require different interface states.
Return behaviorThe delayed reveal creates a natural reason to revisit.
System thinkingThe product combines interface, rules and timing rather than isolated pages.
